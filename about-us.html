Who is Hista Digital?

Since 2021 we bring trained skilled workers into the trades

Our connection to the trades comes from family. My father is a tradesman himself. So I saw early on what it means for a business when people are missing.

For Benedikt it was his grandfather with his own electrical business, which is why hiring good people was always a topic. Benedikt and I aren't just business partners; we have known each other since school.

The connection to the trades and the way people work there stays with us to this day: honest, results-driven and, above all, at eye level. That is exactly how we have worked with our partner businesses for more than five years.

Hista is today one of the leading recruiting providers for the trades, working internationally.
Szymon “Maks” Lebek · Managing Director
