Frequently asked

What businesses ask us before the first call

The answers come from more than 116 collaborations, not from a brochure.

Your question isn't here?Request a callback
How much time does a collaboration take?
A collaboration consists of a welcome call (approx. 30 minutes) and a deep-dive call (approx. 30 minutes). If needed we do a photo shoot, which takes about two to three hours on site. After that you only need time for interviews.
What investment should I expect?
A recruiting service isn't tangible. Many providers exploit that and sell solutions for five-figure sums. Such an investment isn't necessary for one job profile. We name an exact price once we have looked at your case in the consultation call.
How fast do I get the first applications?
After the welcome call we need about 10 to 15 working days for implementation. After campaign start, 92 % of our clients receive qualified applications within the first five days.
I've already burned money. What do you do differently?
Clients often come to us after burning five-figure sums with other agencies. Kolb Industriekessel Service GmbH, for example, had been unsuccessful with a market-leading agency before. We offer the employee what he really wants: an improvement of his professional and private situation.
Where do candidates come from if the market is empty?
Only 5 % of all employees actively look for a new employer. With our motive-based system we move candidates who are currently employed to apply. That is why the method also works when the market is empty.
What if I don't offer special benefits?
Nobody has a magic formula. Our partner businesses didn't hire because they pay outrageous salaries or offer a four-day week. Many employers underestimate themselves. Pay on time and a boss who doesn't shout over every little thing is often all employees want.
How do you know which employee fits me?
In the deep-dive call we go into your exact requirements in depth instead of staying on the surface: training, driving licence, availability, region.
